# Career & Getting Hired — Master Checklist

This is the **track-level** checklist. Phase checklists live inside the phase files. These items should be true of you **when the whole track is done** — and several of them are things you **do**, not things you know, which is the difference between this track and the other nine.

> **Status note.** This track is **not started**: none of its four phases are written yet. This checklist is the **target**, published now so you can see what the track is aiming at — and so you can start accumulating evidence before the phases land, which is what the track itself recommends.

**Tick only what is actually true.** This is the track where overstating is most tempting and most costly. A checklist you filled in optimistically is a rehearsal for a portfolio you cannot defend, and the interview will find it.

---

## Reading the landscape honestly

- [ ] I can explain what employers are selecting for now that generation is cheap, and why judgement and verification matter more than generation. <!-- id: career-master-c01 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can name the roles that exist around these systems and say what the day actually looks like in each. <!-- id: career-master-c02 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can state which roles a beginner can realistically enter, and what the actual barrier is for the ones that are harder. <!-- id: career-master-c03 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can read a job post and identify what it actually requires, separate from title inflation. <!-- id: career-master-c04 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can say what remote work from the Philippines realistically involves, including engagement type and payment. <!-- id: career-master-c05 energy: normal -->
- [ ] I know what not to chase: leaderboard trivia and framework-of-the-month. <!-- id: career-master-c06 energy: normal -->

## The portfolio

- [ ] I have **one deep project** rather than several shallow ones, and I can say why that is the right shape. <!-- id: career-master-c07 energy: high -->
- [ ] My best project includes an evaluation suite, not only a working demo. <!-- id: career-master-c08 energy: high -->
- [ ] I have assembled the deliverables from this curriculum into a portfolio rather than starting from nothing. <!-- id: career-master-c09 energy: high -->
- [ ] I have written a project writeup covering problem, approach, what broke, how I knew it worked, and what I would do differently. <!-- id: career-master-c10 energy: high -->
- [ ] My writeup shows my **reasoning**, not only my result. <!-- id: career-master-c11 energy: high -->
- [ ] My project runs on a clean machine, with pinned dependencies and no secrets. <!-- id: career-master-c12 energy: high -->
- [ ] My commit history is honest and readable. <!-- id: career-master-c13 energy: normal -->
- [ ] I have removed anything I cannot explain, including tutorial clones. <!-- id: career-master-c14 energy: normal -->
- [ ] **I have applied the "could I defend this in an interview?" test to every project I show.** <!-- id: career-master-c15 energy: high -->
- [ ] Every project I show states at least one honest limitation. <!-- id: career-master-c16 energy: high -->

## Evidence without a job

- [ ] I have made a contribution to an open-source project, at an honest and appropriate scale. <!-- id: career-master-c17 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can read unfamiliar code well enough to find where a change belongs. <!-- id: career-master-c18 energy: high -->
- [ ] I have written a technical post explaining one mechanism I learned. <!-- id: career-master-c19 energy: high -->
- [ ] I have built something for a real user — even one. <!-- id: career-master-c20 energy: high -->
- [ ] I have reproduced or extended a paper or benchmark. <!-- id: career-master-c21 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can explain why writing an explanation is one of the fastest ways to find my own gaps. <!-- id: career-master-c22 energy: normal -->
- [ ] **I have published a writeup of a bug I found in my own work.** <!-- id: career-master-c23 energy: high -->
- [ ] I participate in communities without spamming them. <!-- id: career-master-c24 energy: normal -->

## Interviewing

- [ ] I can design an LLM feature end to end when asked: retrieval, evaluation, cost, and failure handling. <!-- id: career-master-c25 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can debug a broken prompt or pipeline out loud, explaining my reasoning as I go. <!-- id: career-master-c26 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can explain a mechanism precisely, at whatever depth the questioner wants. <!-- id: career-master-c27 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can reason about cost in an interview without reaching for a specific price. <!-- id: career-master-c28 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can say "I do not know, and here is how I would find out" — and mean it. <!-- id: career-master-c29 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can use AI tools in a take-home without hiding it and without depending on them fatally. <!-- id: career-master-c30 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can explain my own past projects under questioning, including their limitations. <!-- id: career-master-c31 energy: high -->
- [ ] I can frame my background as a career changer rather than apologising for it. <!-- id: career-master-c32 energy: high -->

## The first ninety days

- [ ] **I have a concrete, dated ninety-day plan that starts the day I am hired.** <!-- id: career-master-c33 energy: high -->
- [ ] I know what I would learn on the job first, and why that order. <!-- id: career-master-c34 energy: normal -->
- [ ] I know how I would ask for help without stalling. <!-- id: career-master-c35 energy: normal -->
- [ ] I intend to ship small and build trust before proposing anything large. <!-- id: career-master-c36 energy: normal -->
- [ ] I intend to keep a work log. <!-- id: career-master-c37 energy: low -->
- [ ] I intend to keep evaluating rather than drifting back to vibes. <!-- id: career-master-c38 energy: high -->

## What this checklist is not

It is not a promise of employment, and nothing here can be. The market is noisy, timing matters, and this track deliberately teaches **procedures rather than statistics** because job-market numbers go stale faster than anything else in the curriculum.

It is a statement that **you have evidence, you can explain it, and you can defend it under questioning.** That is the part within your control, and it is the part this track exists to build.

**Two items to prioritise above the rest.** `career-master-c08` — a project with an evaluation suite — is the single most legible artifact a junior can produce, for the reason the track states plainly: one deep project with measurement and an admitted limitation beats five demos. And `career-master-c23` — publishing a bug you found in your own work — costs nothing, takes an afternoon, and demonstrates exactly the judgement that is scarce.

**If you are job-hunting now**, do not wait for the phases. Start `career-master-c07`, `c10` and `c16` today with whatever you have already built.
