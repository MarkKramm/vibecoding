# Weekly Tracker Template

Copy this into a new file each week, or keep one file and edit it in place. Two minutes of planning on Monday saves twenty minutes of deciding what to do on Wednesday.

The point is not record-keeping. The point is that **"what should I do today?" should not be a question you answer from scratch every day** — that decision costs attention you came here to spend on the material.

## How to use this without it becoming a chore

**Fill in Monday's row on Monday, not Sunday.** A tracker completed retrospectively is a fiction, and it will show you a streak you did not have.

**"Time spent" should be honest, including the distracted minutes.** A log that always says 120 minutes is a log you have stopped reading, and once you stop reading it the whole thing is decoration.

**If a week goes badly, write down that it went badly.** The bad weeks are the ones with information in them. A tracker that only records good weeks cannot tell you what a bad one looked like, which is exactly what you need at the moment you are trying to avoid a second one.

**Keep the last four weeks.** After a month, read back through and look for the pattern in the "what I had to look up twice" answers. Something recurring there is a phase worth revisiting properly rather than patching each time it appears.
